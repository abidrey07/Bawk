# Full-scale Bawk roadmap

- AI drafts problem sets for BCBA review and editing before assignment.
- AI generates supportive feedback and assists review of typed or dictated open-ended responses, with supervisor oversight.
- Analyze goal graphs against the BCBA-entered mastery criteria and propose mastery for human confirmation.
- Replace the static chicken icon on questions with responsive chicken characters and animations.
- Production cloud synchronization, identity/access management, audit history, encrypted transport, and secure clinical data handling.

These are future features, not implemented AI behavior in this local prototype. No AI keys or calls are required for the current version.
